Available on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

What it does

  • Dislikes, back in place. See the estimate in YouTube’s own dislike button while browsing in Safari.
  • A quick look at the numbers. Open the Safari toolbar popup for likes and estimated dislikes on the current video.
  • Share a video, see its stats. On iPhone and iPad, share a YouTube link to Zorro. Checking a link never submits a vote.
  • Your choice to contribute. New likes and dislikes are sent to Return YouTube Dislike by default. You can turn this off in the extension settings.

What the ≈ means

Dislike counts come from Return YouTube Dislike, an independent, community-powered service. The ≈ marks an estimate, not an official YouTube count. Availability depends on that service and YouTube’s page layout.

How it works

Enable Zorro in Safari and allow access to YouTube and the RYD API. Open a video, and the estimate appears alongside the dislike icon. The Safari extension works on YouTube’s website; in the YouTube app, use Share to check a video with Zorro on iPhone or iPad.

There are no analytics or advertising SDKs. Looking up a video sends its ID to RYD, which also sees your IP address. Contributing votes additionally uses a random installation identifier. Read what is shared and what stays on your device →
