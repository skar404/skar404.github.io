Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Slowth does not collect your browsing history or screen content. The App Store app has no account, analytics, tracking, advertising SDK, or developer-operated backend. Blocking and screen analysis run on your device.

Safari blocking

Slowth is a Safari extension with a companion app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It reads supported pages locally to hide or redirect the content you choose to block. Browsing history, page content, and blocking choices are not sent to the developer.

Real-time Blocking (Beta) — iPhone and iPad

If you enable this optional feature, Apple’s ReplayKit supplies screen frames while the broadcast is active. Slowth uses Core ML on your device to detect selected Shorts, Reels, or Stories and asks iOS to show a Screen Time shield. The system recording indicator remains visible while monitoring is active.

Screen frames are not saved or uploaded during normal use of the App Store app. Audio samples are not processed. You can stop monitoring using the recording controls. Developer capture and upload tools are restricted to debug builds and are not available in the App Store app.

On Mac, Slowth blocks content only in Safari; it does not monitor the screen or block content inside other apps.

What stays on your device

Settings, per-site modes, the Strict Mode timer, app-selection tokens, and local operating status are stored on your device. Downloaded blocking rules are cached locally. These settings and rules are not sent to the developer.

Rule updates and network requests

The Safari extension periodically downloads a public blocking-rule file from GitHub Gist over HTTPS. The same rules are available to everyone. The request does not include browsing history, page content, screen frames, or your app selections.

GitHub receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, when serving the file. Its own privacy policy applies.

Screen Time and Family Controls — iOS only

With your permission, Slowth uses Apple’s Family Controls and Managed Settings frameworks to shield selected apps. Your selections are represented by privacy-protected tokens, stored locally and used to ask iOS to apply the shield.

Website and external services

The Slowth website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Visiting it creates ordinary web requests, so those providers receive connection information such as your IP address. Opening other external links is governed by the destination’s privacy policy. The language links on this page work without cookies or JavaScript.

Children

Slowth does not collect browsing history or screen content from users, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Material changes will also be described in app release notes.
