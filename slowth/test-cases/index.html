Data safety

We store files in private Backblaze B2 storage. We use them to train and test the Slowth neural network.

We do not publish or sell your files, or share them with other companies for their use.

You can delete a file yourself within one hour of uploading. After that, request deletion by emailing denis@malina.page and include your visitor ID: ….

BEFORE RECORDING

What to record

  1. Short videos.

    Open Reels, Shorts, TikTok, or a similar feed. Scroll through a few videos.

  2. Normal app use.

    Show a post feed, profile, search, or long video. These examples help Slowth avoid blocking normal content.

  3. Upload your files.

    Record for 15–60 seconds or take full-screen screenshots. You can upload multiple files and optionally label their apps and content types.

Record the whole screen without cropping. Turn on Do Not Disturb and avoid showing personal information.
